Common questions

How much is a pack of cigarettes in Malta?

About €5.71 for a pack of 20 (typical price, October 2026).

How much does one cigarette cost in Malta?

About €0.29, with a pack of 20 at €5.71.

How much does smoking cost a year in Malta?

A pack a day costs about €173.81 a month and €2,086 a year. Half a pack a day costs about €1,043 a year.
